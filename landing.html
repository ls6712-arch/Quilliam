Meet Quilliam

He praises real ink and roasts robot mush

Quilliam watches how a letter gets written: the pauses, the crossings-out, the wobbly loops. Pasted paragraphs and chatbot phrases move his meter toward “robot slop.”

  • His stamp is earned from how you wrote, never from a guess about your words.

Questions

Asked by humans

How does Quilliam know a person wrote it?

He watches how the letter is made, not just what it says. Pen strokes, typing rhythm, pauses, backspaces and pasted text all count. A letter written stroke by stroke earns the stamp; a pasted block of text does not. Because the stamp comes from those signals, it doesn’t rely on guessing whether words “sound like AI.”

Does my recipient need an account?

No. They open the link in any browser and see your letter, the seal and Quilliam’s stamp. They can save it or write one back, and their first letter is free too.

Can I type instead of handwriting?

Yes. Typed letters can still earn the stamp when they’re typed, not pasted. Handwriting is the default because it’s the strongest proof and the most personal.

Who can see my letter?

Anyone with its link, so share it the way you’d share a photo. Links are long and random, and letters aren’t listed or searchable anywhere.

Do Holiday Pack letters expire?

No. Pack letters stay with the browser you bought them in until you use them. The Season Pass is unlimited until midnight on January 1 (New York time).

Can my company use its own branding?

Yes. The Company layout takes your logo, brand color and font, and can turn your logo into the wax seal. Request a quote above for volume pricing and invoicing.
